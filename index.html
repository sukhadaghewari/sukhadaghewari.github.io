About

I am an applied machine learning researcher and engineer working on climate, Earth observation, and health. Right now I work on extreme weather prediction, focusing on tropical cyclones, early warning, and satellite imagery. I like finding out whether an idea actually works, then building it into something people use, which is what draws me to applied interdisciplinary research.

I did my MS in Computer Science at UC San Diego and am now based in India. Before this, I built LLM systems for healthcare at Oracle and machine learning for cybersecurity at TCS Research. I also have a background in clinical NLP and AI fairness.
I am currently applying to PhD programs and I am also open to research roles.
